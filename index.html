The Game

Make It Count is a top-down puzzle action game where one bullet is all you get. Players must carefully plan each shot, using ricochets, environmental objects, and chain reactions to eliminate every target in the room. Every decision matters because there is no room for wasted shots.

Gameplay

Each room presents the player with a new puzzle. Before firing, players need to study the environment and determine how their bullet will travel.

Ricochets allow players to redirect their bullet around the environment and reach targets that may not be directly in front of them. Finding the correct angle is an important part of solving each room.

Contracts provide the player with their missions and give each level a specific objective to complete.

Successfully completing a room allows the player to progress through the available levels and take on more challenging puzzles.

Future Development

One of the main areas we would like to develop further is the story behind Make It Count. The current version focuses mainly on the core gameplay, but a future version could introduce a stronger narrative through contracts, objectives, and context for each mission. This would give the player a greater reason to progress through the game and make each contract feel more meaningful.

We would also continue improving the collision and ricochet systems. Making collisions more consistent and refining how bullets interact with walls and objects would make shots feel smoother and give players more precise control when planning their solutions.
